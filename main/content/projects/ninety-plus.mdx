# Ninety+

A modern football analytics platform delivering live Premier League matchday standings, dynamic club insights, fixtures, and news tracking with an ultra-clean UI.

## Overview

Designed for football enthusiasts and data-driven fans who want instant access to matchday dynamics without bloated sports portals and sluggish ad-filled interfaces.

---

## Key Features

- **Live Standings & Matchday Telemetry**: Real-time points, goal differences, forms, and fixtures with automated rank tracking.
- **Club & Player Deep-Dives**: Detailed stats, squad profiles, and recent form guides.
- **Responsive Dark/Light UI**: Built with Next.js App Router and Tailwind CSS for instant page transitions and high accessibility.
